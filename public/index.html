OUR MISSION

"To create technology and experiences that help people hold onto what matters most — identity, voice, love, and connection."

HuLux Innovations exists to reimagine how technology supports humanity — not by replacing care, but by deepening it. Our work sits at the intersection of human connection, memory, caregiving, and human-centered design.

WHY CONNECTION

The thing technology forgot to protect.

Connection is one of the most powerful forces in a human life. It shapes our wellbeing, our resilience, our sense of identity and belonging. It carries us through caregiving, through learning, through aging, through grief.

And yet almost no technology is built to strengthen it. Most is built to capture our attention, or to stand in for the people we'd rather be with. The result is a world more connected than ever — and somehow lonelier.

HuLux exists to build the other kind of technology: the kind that gives people back to each other.

Technology should deepen humanity, not replace it.

LEADERSHIP

Built by someone who has been in the room.

HuLux Innovations was founded by Lisette Diaz Santiago, a healthcare leader, educator, and program builder with more than 14 years across healthcare and education — including over a decade inside one of the country's largest safety-net health systems.

At Boston Medical Center, Lisette's career moved through the work itself — from coordinating a high-acuity Surgical ICU, to managing project operations in Trauma & Acute Care Surgery, to leading patient experience initiatives that improved HCAHPS scores and built unit-level champion programs, to ultimately designing and running system-wide training as Learning & Development Manager. As the most seasoned BMC team member in the group rebuilding new-hire onboarding, she shaped much of the orientation curriculum and personally facilitated more than a dozen cohorts — part of a team effort that reached a 98% satisfaction rate across a diverse frontline workforce.

In 2025, she lost her grandfather to progressive dementia. What she learned in those final months — about the moments families search for, the technology they couldn't find, and the parts of a person that remain even when memory recedes — became the foundation for HuLux Innovations, formally established in November 2025.

In summer 2026, Lisette will present at the Alzheimer's Association International Conference (AAIC) on voice-based approaches to supporting emotional connection in dementia care — work drawn directly from MemoriGems' early thinking.

HuLux Innovations exists because she could not find what her family needed — and because she believes connection is worth building for, deliberately and with dignity, so that other families never have to search alone.

Lisette Diaz Santiago
