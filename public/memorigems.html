MemoriGems®

Because connection is more than memory.

A voice-first platform that pairs familiar voices with meaningful images — prioritizing emotional recognition over memory performance for people living with early-stage dementia and the families who love them.

Overview

Designed around what remains.

Rather than asking someone to remember a fact or recognize a face, MemoriGems leads with the familiar voice — a laugh, a story, a phrase only family would say — because emotional and auditory pathways can stay meaningful even as memory changes.

Each "Gem" is a short, voice-first message paired with a meaningful image, designed for low cognitive load and built to be revisited as a ritual that sustains connection over time.

MemoriGems is the first application of VOCES — not its endpoint.

Familiar voice — auditory input Auditory cortex — auditory processing Amygdala — emotional processing

"I feel this" — emotion can connect even when memory fades.

Photos ask the brain to remember. Voice helps the brain feel — even when memory fades.

What it is

A novel, relationship-centered approach that complements clinical care by prioritizing emotional connection through accessible, voice-first design.

What it's not

Not a caregiver replacement, a clinical decision-making tool, or a diagnostic instrument.

Research & Evidence

Evidence-informed by design.

MemoriGems was intentionally designed to align with research on emotional memory and auditory processing. Early observations from caregiver use suggest increased emotional engagement, improved responsiveness, and reduced frustration during everyday communication.

MemoriGems' design is informed by — not validated by — the research it draws upon, and has not yet been studied in clinical trials. We are actively seeking research partners to measure emotional engagement and connection outcomes.
